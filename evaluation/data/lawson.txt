9. PROJECT TITLE
Assessment and Prioritization of Barriers to Connectivity in the Uchee Creek Watershed (AL)
10. PROJECT DESCRIPTION
Conduct Sediment Risk Index assessments for road-stream crossings in Alabama's Uchee Watershed and survey biological assemblages at high and moderate risk sites to identify barriers to connectivity, prioritizing sites for remediation. Project will assess 500 barriers and provide a catalyst for conservation activity in this watershed to benefit aquatic species of greatest conservation need and to build relationships with the community through land stewardship education.
